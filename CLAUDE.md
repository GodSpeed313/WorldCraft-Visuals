## UI/UX

- **Frontend:** static HTML/CSS/JS in a single file, `dashboard.html`. No framework. Tailwind is not used.
- **ui-ux-pro-max:** leave `--stack` unset. Use only the general searches (color, typography, accessibility/ux, chart).
- **Fonts:** Syne and Space Mono (Google Fonts) are established. Typography results must not override them.
- **Advisory only:** UI/UX suggestions are recommendations. Do not edit `dashboard.html` without explicit operator approval.
