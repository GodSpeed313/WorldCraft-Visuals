WorldCraft Proposal 001 — Compositional Capability Ontology

Type: PROPOSAL. Not a ruling, not a contract, not a gap. Rules nothing, closes nothing, authorizes no edit to any registry, code file, or governing document.

Provenance. Originates in an external conversation between the operator and a different model (ChatGPT), conducted outside this corpus and pasted into the WorldCraft session on 2026-08-30. It is recorded here under the same logic Ruling 001 §5 applies to any model output: the model proposes, the corpus decides. Nothing in this document carries authority merely because it is written down. It is preserved so that when the corpus is ready to rule on the questions it touches, the questions are not re-derived from scratch and the bridge is not silently re-crossed.

Why a standalone file rather than an entry in open_contract_gaps.md. The register closes entries against a stated condition — a Contract 001 invariant, or a document claiming a named topic. This proposal does not state a condition of that shape for most of what it raises; it is closer to a sketched architecture than a bounded requirement. Filing it as a proposal record, rather than forcing it into gap-register shape, avoids manufacturing false precision about what kind of thing it is.

Citation convention. Per audit_method.md M8, references to this document from elsewhere in the corpus must be filename-qualified (proposal_001_compositional_capability_ontology.md:NNN) — a bare :NNN anchor resolves to contract_001_domain_resolution.md by house convention (M3) and would misresolve here.

1. What the conversation actually proposes

Compressed to its structural claims, independent of the conversation's own framing or examples:

P1 — A scale/scope axis, orthogonal to modality and family. "What a capability does" and "how far it reaches" are proposed as separate fields, so that teleportation-across-a-room and teleportation-across-dimensions are one capability at two scales, not two unrelated registry entries.

P2 — Capability / Expression / Scale / Modality as four distinct layers, where the current registry conflates at least the first three into a single family + min_modality pair per power.

P3 — Power systems (Ki, Chakra, Nen, Devil Fruits, Haki, etc.) as their own compositional sub-taxonomies, not flat registry entries — each with an internal System → Mechanism → Technique → Effect → Scale → Modality shape, rather than one POWER_REGISTRY row per named mechanic.

P4 — A top-level ontology above the current registry, sketched as Entities / Attributes / Power Systems / Power Registry, under which POWER_REGISTRY becomes one subsystem rather than the whole model.

P5 — Entity/Attribute/Source/Capability as distinct kinds, motivated by the observation that CHARACTER_REGISTRY entries (a historical figure, an actor's role, a persona, a fictional character) are not the same kind of thing even when they share a name, and collapsing them loses information the same way Ruling 001 §3.3 already holds that collapsing alias/derivative/related loses information for powers.

P6 — Unknown-capability inference with explicit UNVERIFIED status — proposing inferred capability, family, modality, and scale fields for an unrecognized power, surfaced to the operator for confirmation rather than silently defaulted.

P7 — An explicit argument against manually registering the full space of source material (every anime character, actor, philosopher, historical figure) in favor of the compositional model absorbing them structurally instead.

This section is the reason to preserve the document: several of P1–P7 are not new questions. They are candidate answers to questions already on record as open, and one is genuinely new territory no document has claimed.

New territory, structurally analogous to an existing solved problem. §3.3 solved this exact shape of problem (don't collapse distinct relationships into one identity) for powers. P6 is not a new idea — it is a restatement of the resolver Ruling 001 §5 and Ruling 002 §8 already require and that has never been implemented. Its only actual addition is the scale field, which inherits P1's status above.
P7 (argument against manual registration at scale)    Ruling 001 §1 — "minting is cheap, merging is expensive"; the present session's live discussion of hand-authoring Hashirama Senju / William Vangeance    Consistent with existing philosophy, not a new principle. Cheap minting was always meant to scale via the unbuilt resolver (P6/Ruling 001 §5), not via manual authorship. P7 correctly names the wall the corpus already anticipated and has not yet built past.
3. What this document explicitly does not do
It does not open a gap. Nothing here is filed against open_contract_gaps.md, no gap number is claimed or reserved, and the register's closure rules are untouched.
It does not amend, correct, or supersede any locked document. Ruling 001, Ruling 002, and Contract 001 stand exactly as signed.
It does not adopt P4's domain list, or any other specific taxonomy named above. Where a point overlaps a live open question (10.1, 10.3, 10.9), this document records the overlap and explicitly declines to resolve it here.
It does not authorize any registry, engine, or schema change. POWER_REGISTRY, CHARACTER_REGISTRY, TRANSPOSITION_MAP, and every other implementation file are untouched by this proposal's existence.
It does not sequence itself ahead of anything currently in progress. Ruling 002 §10.5 (the ten-entry reclassification) and the successor contract (GAP-4/GAP-5) proceed independently of this document and are not blocked by it, nor does this document claim priority over them.
4. What this document is for

A dated, citable record that the questions in §2's left column were considered together, from an external source, before the corpus took up 10.1, 10.3, 10.9, or any future power-systems / entity- modeling gap. When the corpus is ready to rule on any of those, this document is the place to start — not to re-litigate whether the questions exist, but to decide what to do about them.
