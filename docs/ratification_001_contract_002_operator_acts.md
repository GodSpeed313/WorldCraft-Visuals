# WorldCraft Ratification 001 — Contract 002 Operator Acts

**Type:** RATIFICATION. A new instrument type in this corpus, meaning here — and stated expressly
because the ordinary sense of the word can mislead — **retrospective confirmation and archival of
operator acts that already possessed independent legal force, not the act that supplies, completes,
or perfects that force.** Contract 002's governing status is complete and independent of this
instrument: it became binding through its own DRAFT → SIGNED → LOCKED lifecycle, individually
attested §-by-§ by the operator, entirely on its own terms. This Ratification adds nothing to that
status and subtracts nothing from it. It is not a ruling, not a contract, not a gap, not a
correction, not an authorization, not a disposition, not a proposal. It closes no register entry,
authorizes no edit to any code or document, and does not reopen, amend, or supersede any locked
text, including Contract 002 itself.

**Why this instrument exists, and why now.** An audit conducted 2026-09-15, after Contract 002
reached LOCKED status, found that two statements inside Contract 002's own text — that a further
explicit operator act lifted the successor-contract hold and opened Contract 002's governance
process, and that the operator explicitly determined I8(a)'s terminal-halting scope should include
`AMBIGUOUS_DOMAIN` alongside the states Disposition 002 §2.4 names — are presently evidenced only
inside Contract 002 itself. No separate, independently-discoverable corpus artifact records either
act. `authorizations.md`'s own founding reason applies exactly here: *"an authorization that lives
only in a chat turn ... is not discoverable later by anyone reading the corpus."*

**On chronology — stated conservatively, because that is what the evidence supports.** The only
independently-verifiable timestamp in the durable corpus is the commit that landed Contract 002's
finished, LOCKED content (`42427a6`, 2026-09-15 13:30:24 -0400), which establishes only that both
acts, if genuine, preceded that commit. No durable artifact establishes a finer sequence. This
instrument does not claim one. Both acts are recorded below as having occurred **during the Contract
002 governance process, on 2026-09-15, as operator determinations relied upon by the instrument
before its final SIGNED/LOCKED state** — and no more specifically than that.

**What this instrument is not, stated plainly.**

- **It is not new authority, and it does not create, complete, or perfect Contract 002's governing
  force.** See the Type line above. Both acts recorded below already occurred during the Contract
  002 governance process, before its final SIGNED/LOCKED state — this instrument does not
  authorize, determine, or decide anything now; it independently confirms that Kevin already did.
- **It does not amend Contract 002.** Contract 002 is LOCKED; `amendment_policy.md` §1 forecloses
  any edit to it, without exception, including to repair a defect the corpus has itself recorded.
  Contract 002's text stays exactly as signed. This instrument supplies what was missing
  *alongside* it, not a correction *to* it.
- **It does not backdate itself.** This instrument is dated 2026-09-15, created after Contract 002's
  LOCK, and says so. It does not claim to have existed before Contract 002 was drafted, signed, or
  locked, and no reader should infer that it did.
- **It does not reopen GAP-4** or alter its CLOSED status in `open_contract_gaps.md`. GAP-4's closure
  rested on Contract 002's substantive content (I8's satisfaction of the operative closure
  condition), which this instrument does not change.
- **It does not authorize implementation, open Seam 2, or touch GAP-5.**

## 1. The successor-contract hold-lifting and process-opening act

**What actually opened the governance process, and what this entry is.** These are two different
things, exactly as AUTH-003 and AUTH-004 already distinguish for this corpus:

- **The operator act** — Kevin's own determination, made during the Contract 002 governance
  process, on 2026-09-15, before Contract 002's final SIGNED/LOCKED state, narrowing the FE
  Requirements §39 "Successor contract — ON HOLD" item specifically to permit beginning the
  Contract 002 governance process (drafting, review, and eventual sign-off of a successor
  instrument to Contract 001 §6) — is what has legal force. It does not derive its authority from
  this instrument, or from any register.
- **This Ratification entry** is the archive of that act — recorded here because, as of this
  audit, it existed nowhere else in discoverable form.

**Scope.** This entry confirms only that the hold was narrowed to permit *beginning the governance
process* — drafting and reviewing a successor instrument. It does not itself authorize
implementation of anything that instrument would come to require; FE Requirements §39's remaining
holds (existing code UNMODIFIED, etc.) are unaffected and stand exactly as recorded.

**Consequence for FE Requirements §39.** `fusion_engine_requirements_v0.1.md` is LOCKED and its §39
line cannot be edited. This entry does not attempt to. The line *"Successor contract — ON HOLD"*
remains on the page exactly as attested, true as of its own date; this Ratification records that
the hold was subsequently narrowed by the act above, without touching that document.

## 2. The AMBIGUOUS_DOMAIN terminal-halting determination

**What actually extended I8(a)'s scope, and what this entry is.**

- **The operator act** — Kevin's own determination, made during the Contract 002 governance
  process, on 2026-09-15, before Contract 002's final SIGNED/LOCKED state, that GAP-4's requirement
  that terminal conditions be halting rather than advisory applies consistently across the
  resolution sequence, including `AMBIGUOUS_DOMAIN` alongside `UNRESOLVED_DOMAIN` and
  `UNRESOLVED_FAMILY` — the two states Disposition 002 §2.4 actually names — is what has legal
  force.
- **This Ratification entry** is the archive of that determination, exactly as Disposition 001 and
  Disposition 002 archive the operator's other substantive determinations, distinguished from a
  Disposition only by being recorded after, rather than before, the text that relied on it was
  drafted.

**Relationship to Disposition 002.** This entry does not amend, correct, or reopen Disposition 002.
Disposition 002 §2.4 remains exactly as recorded — a determination scoped to `UNRESOLVED_DOMAIN` and
`UNRESOLVED_FAMILY`. This entry records a *further*, separate operator determination, consistent
with — not contradicting — Disposition 002's own stated basis (that terminal conditions must
actually halt, not merely advise), extending that basis to the one further state Disposition 002
itself did not name.

**What this does not decide.** This entry does not determine that any other state, present or
future, is similarly halting. It confirms only the specific extension to `AMBIGUOUS_DOMAIN` that
Contract 002 I8(a) already states.
