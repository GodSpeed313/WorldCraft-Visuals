## 2. Determination

1. **Electromagnetic Pulse remains the priority case**, per §10.5's own designation
   (`ruling_002_family_taxonomy_integrity.md:538-539`).
2. **No replacement family or domain is assigned** to Electromagnetic Pulse, or to any of the other
   nine mis-fitted entries, before §2.3's precondition is met. This is the substantive question §1
   holds open: closing the order-and-method question does not supply, or bring closer to being
   supplied, any of the ten's eventual classification. The lookup table at
   `scratch_ruling002_10.5_worksheet.md` (untracked, data-gathering only) remains a worksheet, not a
   classification, and is unaffected by this determination.
3. **Precondition.** Domain-taxonomy / machine-enforceable architecture sufficient to classify
   Electromagnetic Pulse (and the other nine) honestly must exist before any of the ten receives a
   landing family or domain. What satisfies this precondition is not decided here.
4. **Instrument for the eventual reclassification.** Once §2.3 is met, the ten-entry reclassification
   is carried out through a dedicated ruling-level instrument — not inline registry edits, a
   correction entry, or an authorization alone. That instrument, not this one, closes the substantive
   question §1 leaves open.
5. **Kinetic Mastery is treated independently.** Its presence in the audit's set of ten (family axis,
   §10.5) does not carry an assumption that its family must change. Its family question (§10.5) stays
   distinct from its modality question (§10.7), per §10.5's own axis split
   (`ruling_002_family_taxonomy_integrity.md:541-544`).
6. **Unresolved cases stay unresolved** where the evidence does not distinguish them — including
   Cursed Energy's dual-cluster status, Spiral Power's domain-unresolved placement, and the §10.9
   n=1 candidate clusters (Titan-Shifting, Equivalent Exchange, Kinetic Mastery).
