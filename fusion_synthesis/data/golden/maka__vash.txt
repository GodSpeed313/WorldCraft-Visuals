EMERGENT ABILITY
  Mercy Frequency  [INVENTED_BRIDGE]
  A tuned release: the user perceives each soul in range and sends the discharge as threads matched to chosen targets, disabling weapons and breaking corrupting resonance while sparing the living.
  governing rule (INVENTED_BRIDGE): Soul perception maps every living soul in range; the stored discharge is released as narrow threads tuned to a chosen set of targets, sized to disable weapons and break corrupting resonance rather than lives, and the vow caps the output below lethal.
  derived from: maka.soul_perception, vash.angel_arm, vash.arm_output_limit, vash.pacifist_vow
  activation: Sustained perception of every target in range.
  limitations:
    - Cannot target what cannot be perceived: out of perception range, or against a soul too shielded or disguised to read, the release does not fire.
    - Needs unbroken concentration: any break in perception collapses the tuning and the release is lost, never fired untuned.
    - Only a handful of targets can be tuned at once; a crowd of aggressors exceeds what one release can hold.
    - Each full release is a heavy strain: perception range shrinks for a while afterwards.
    - The vow bounds the output: the ability has no lethal setting, so it cannot end a fight that only a lethal blow would end.
  inherited limitations:
    - Arm Output Limit [Vash] (hard limitation): The arm's discharge is enormous and indiscriminate; it is a last resort because it cannot be fine-tuned.
    - Pacifist Vow [Vash] (prohibition): Refuses to take a life, even an enemy's.

EXCLUSIONS
  - the literal weapon partner as a carried companion [A: Maka]: A separate person; only the partner's channel-shaping role is carried, as an instrument.
  - the academy setting and its hierarchy [A: Maka]: Setting and institution, not a mechanic.
  - the arm's unrestrained destructive output [B: Vash]: Excluded by the vow: there is no setting that releases the discharge untuned.
  - bounty and legend status [B: Vash]: A reputation is a narrative role, not a transferable mechanic.
