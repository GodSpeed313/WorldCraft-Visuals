EMERGENT ABILITY
  Passing Cover  [DEFENSIBLY_DERIVED]
  After watching a place's customs, the user assumes a convincing local role built from them, holding it for the length of a short stay and leaving no trace when it ends.
  governing rule (DEFENSIBLY_DERIVED): Observation of a place's customs is fed straight into covert tradecraft to build a short-lived, convincing local role, and the three-day rule bounds how long that role can hold; the whole of it stays within human limits.
  derived from: kino.witness_stance, james_bond.covert_tradecraft, kino.three_day_rule, james_bond.human_limits
  activation: Time spent watching the place's customs before committing to a role.
  limitations:
    - Needs watching first: a role built without observing the place's customs fails at the first test.
    - It holds for about three days; after that, repeated contact exposes the seams and the user must be gone.
    - It is ordinary skill, not disguise magic: a close acquaintance, an official check or a trained eye can break it.
    - Human limits apply: injury, fatigue and fear are all real, and a role does not protect the body.
    - One role at a time: two stories in the same place collapse each other.
  inherited limitations:
    - Three Day Rule [Kino] (prohibition): Never stays in any one country longer than three days.
    - Human Limits [James Bond] (hard limitation): Injury, exhaustion, poison and fear all apply; no powers beyond human skill.
