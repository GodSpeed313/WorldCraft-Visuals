EMERGENT ABILITY
  Quiet Volley  [DEFENSIBLY_DERIVED]
  After watching a dispute long enough to read who holds what and how each will move, the user ends it with one fast, precise volley that strips every weapon from every armed hand without killing anyone.
  governing rule (DEFENSIBLY_DERIVED): Time spent watching a dispute teaches the user which hands hold weapons and in what order they will move; one fast volley then disables every weapon in a single breath, the vow keeps every shot non-lethal, and the three-day rule forces the user to leave before the place can adapt.
  derived from: kino.witness_stance, kino.quick_draw, vash.sharpshooting, vash.pacifist_vow, kino.three_day_rule
  activation: Sustained observation of a dispute, with the user not yet committed to either side.
  limitations:
    - Needs watching first: without time spent observing a dispute, the volley cannot be aimed and does not fire.
    - It only reaches weapons held in a hand; it cannot stop a threat that is not held, such as gas, fire or a locked room.
    - Past a handful of armed targets, one volley cannot disable them all and is not attempted.
    - The vow bounds it: no setting is lethal, so it cannot end a fight that only a lethal shot would end.
    - The three-day rule applies: the user must leave before the third day ends, so a dispute that outlasts the stay is left unresolved.
  inherited limitations:
    - Pacifist Vow [Vash] (prohibition): Refuses to take a life, even an enemy's.
    - Three Day Rule [Kino] (prohibition): Never stays in any one country longer than three days.
