EMERGENT ABILITY
  Threefold Finding  [INVENTED_BRIDGE]
  The user reconstructs an event in three separate records, its form (what physically happened), its truth (what was done and by whom) and its reason (why), and only when all three agree may the cause be sealed with a paper talisman, ending the harm without punishing anyone.
  governing rule (INVENTED_BRIDGE): Forensic method establishes the form and the truth of an event from evidence; spirit discernment supplies the reason as a reading of the unseen cause, weighed as testimony; the threefold condition makes all three records mandatory before the user may seal the cause with a paper talisman, a binding that stops the harm without punishing anyone; the user's official standing bounds who is bound to honour the result.
  derived from: the_medicine_seller.spirit_discernment, the_medicine_seller.threefold_knowing, re_l_mayer.forensic_method, the_medicine_seller.warding_talismans, re_l_mayer.standing_boundary
  activation: Time and access to evidence, testimony and the place where the harm began.
  limitations:
    - All three records are required: with the form, the truth or the reason missing or in conflict, nothing can be alleged or sealed.
    - The reason is a reading, not proof: it is weighed as testimony and never stands alone as evidence.
    - Sealing stops a cause, not a consequence: harm already done is not undone, and nobody is punished or cleared by it.
    - A finding carries weight only where the user has official standing; elsewhere it is advice that may be ignored.
    - Assembling three agreeing records is slow, demanding work that force cannot hurry.
  inherited limitations:
    - Threefold Knowing [The Medicine Seller] (hard limitation): The sealed sword can be drawn only once the Form, the Truth and the Reason of the spirit are all known.
    - Standing Boundary [Re-l Mayer] (hard limitation): Her authority exists only inside the city's institutions; beyond them it carries no weight.
