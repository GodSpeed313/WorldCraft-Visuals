EMERGENT ABILITY
  Residual Reading  [INVENTED_BRIDGE]
  At a scene, the user reads the faint residue souls leave behind and orders it, by forensic method, into a timeline of who was present and in what state, recorded as evidence for a case.
  governing rule (INVENTED_BRIDGE): Soul perception is turned from the living to what they leave behind: faint residues of recent presence are read at a scene and ordered by forensic method into a sequence of who was there and in what state, recorded as evidence for a case and bounded by the user's official standing.
  derived from: maka.soul_perception, re_l_mayer.forensic_method, re_l_mayer.standing_boundary
  activation: Quiet, undisturbed time at the scene, with perception turned to what is left behind.
  limitations:
    - Residue fades: traces older than a short window cannot be read, and a disturbed scene is partly lost.
    - It records presence and state, not intent or guilt; a reading is evidence to be weighed, never a verdict.
    - Artificial persons leave no residue, so anyone or anything without a soul is invisible to it.
    - A reading carries weight only where the user has official standing; outside it, it is an unsupported claim.
    - Overlapping traces are slow, demanding work and leave the user drained.
  inherited limitations:
    - Standing Boundary [Re-l Mayer] (hard limitation): Her authority exists only inside the city's institutions; beyond them it carries no weight.
